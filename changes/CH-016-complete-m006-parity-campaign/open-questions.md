# Open questions

## Melee comparison population

The CAP-005 design explicitly leaves the official melee comparison shape open: ten copies of the subject robot (self-play) or a fixed opponent set. The choice changes what SCORE-006 evidence means and can materially change the observed score and event mix. The existing harness supports the official participant count but does not make this comparison policy explicit. A human decision is required before official melee observations are accepted into the campaign.
